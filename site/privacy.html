Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

What stays on your computer

Your settings, your bet logs, your license key, and the game window's browser profile (including your casino login) are stored only on your computer, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\{{name}}. {{name}} never sends them to us.

What we receive when you subscribe

  • Your machine ID: a code made from your computer's Windows ID by a one-way calculation. It identifies the computer your subscription is for; it reveals nothing else about it.
  • Your MoMo number: to send the payment request to your phone, and to find your payment if you contact us.
  • The payment details: plan, amount, date, status and MTN's transaction reference.

Payments are processed by MTN Mobile Money; we never see your PIN. Orders are stored by our license service, which runs on Cloudflare. We keep order records for as long as we need them for accounting and support, and at most five years.

What the app downloads

When it starts, {{name}} downloads small files from this website: the latest version number, a list of cancelled licenses, and fixes for the game page. Like any website, the web host can see your IP address when this happens.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to delete it once it is no longer needed for accounting.
