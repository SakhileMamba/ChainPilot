Responsible gambling

Gambling should be entertainment you can afford, never a way to make money or pay debts. {{name}} is built with hard limits for that reason, but limits only help if you set them honestly and keep them.

Under {{minimumAge}}?

Gambling and {{name}} are only for adults. Please leave this site.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned, or raising your limits to win back losses.
  • Borrowing money, selling things, or using money meant for bills, food or family to gamble.
  • Feeling restless or irritable when you try to stop.
  • Hiding how much you gamble from people close to you.
  • Believing a system, a "predictor" or a lucky streak will win the money back.

Tools that help

  • Set the loss limit in {{name}} to an amount you can lose without it hurting, and never raise it in the same day.
  • Set a time limit, and take a break after every session.
  • Use your casino's limits: deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion.
  • Keep your bet logs and look at the totals honestly once a week.
