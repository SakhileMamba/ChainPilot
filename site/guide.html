First start

Open {{name}} from the Start menu. The control panel opens in its own window, and a small log window opens minimised; keep it open. The first time, {{name}} asks you to confirm that you are {{minimumAge}} or older and that you understand it doesn't predict rounds or change the odds.

The game window

Press Open game window. {{name}} opens {{game.name}} in a separate Chrome or Edge window with its own profile. Log in there once and the login is remembered. The casino only opens Aviator, even its play-money version, for logged-in players, so every mode needs this. Keep that window open and visible while the bot plays (don't minimise it), and leave the game's bet panels alone: {{name}} uses the first (left) panel and reads its messages.

Your plan

Initial bet
What each chain starts with. A lost chain costs exactly this amount.
Cash out at
The multiplier for the game's Auto Cash Out. {{name}} switches Auto Cash Out on and sets it for you.
Wins per chain
After a win, the whole payout is bet again until this many wins in a row complete the chain. 1 = no compounding.
Stop after … completed chains
The session ends after this many completed chains. 0 = no target (only the limits stop it).
Loss limit
The session stops before its total loss could go past this. Set it to what you can afford to lose.
Time limit
The session stops after this many minutes. 0 = no time limit.
Rounds between bets
After each bet, wait a random 1 to k rounds. 1 = bet every round. It doesn't change the odds.

The box This plan at a glance shows what your settings mean. The strategy guide explains them in depth.

Dry run, practice and live

  • Dry run (free): watches real rounds and "bets" on paper. Nothing is clicked.
  • Practice (free): plays the game's play-money mode, so you can watch {{name}} place bets. It refuses to bet unless the game window is on the play-money page.
  • Live (subscription): real money. {{name}} asks you to confirm before it starts.

While it plays

  • The Session panel shows the running total, bets won, chains completed, the next bet and the time left. Every bet is listed and saved to a spreadsheet file (Help → Bet logs).
  • Stop ends the session after any bet in the air has settled. Auto Cash Out runs on the game's server, so a bet still cashes out at your multiplier even if your computer or internet stops.
  • If your casino balance is too low for the next bet, {{name}} pauses. Add funds if you choose to and it carries on by itself, within your loss limit. Or press Stop.
  • Closing the control panel quits {{name}} (after any bet in the air settles).

Your subscription

Open Subscription in the app, choose a plan, enter your MTN MoMo number and press Pay with MoMo. Approve the prompt on your phone with your PIN; the app activates by itself within seconds. If you pay again before your subscription ends, the new days are added on top.

A subscription belongs to one computer, identified by the machine ID on the Subscription tab. If something went wrong (for example you closed the app before the payment confirmed), press Restore purchase.

Changing computers

Reinstalling {{name}} or Windows on the same computer keeps the same machine ID: install, then press Restore purchase. For a different computer, contact us with both machine IDs and your MoMo payment reference and we'll move your remaining time.

Troubleshooting

"Waiting for Aviator - log in and open the game"
Log in and open Aviator in the game window. {{name}} finds the game by itself.
The game window doesn't open
Install Google Chrome, or choose Microsoft Edge under Help → Settings.
"Couldn't open the Auto tab" or bets aren't placed
Reload the game in the game window. If it keeps happening, the casino may have changed the game. Restart {{name}} to get any fixes; if it still fails, contact us.
I approved the payment but I'm not subscribed
Wait a minute and press Restore purchase. Still nothing? Contact us with your machine ID and the MoMo transaction ID.
"Your computer's date looks wrong"
Set Windows to the correct date and time (Settings → Time & language), or connect to the internet, then restart {{name}}.
