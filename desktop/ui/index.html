    How compounding works

    ChainPilot plays a compounding chain (a positive progression, also called Paroli). You risk a small bet; each time it wins, the whole payout rides on the next round instead of being banked. A run of wins grows the stake fast. A loss ends the chain and costs only the initial bet, because everything above it was that chain's own winnings.

    It's the reverse of doubling after losses (Martingale), which risks more and more of your own money to win back a small amount. Here you only ever risk new money one initial bet at a time. That caps how fast you can lose. It doesn't make you win.

    Lower or higher cash-out?

    A lower multiplier wins more often but grows slowly; a higher one grows fast but rarely completes a chain. The average return per bet is the same at any multiplier. You choose the shape of your results, not their average.

    More wins per chain?

    Each extra step multiplies the payout by your cash-out and the chance by about one half. It also multiplies the average return by the game's 97% once more, so longer chains lose more on average.

    Gaps between bets?

    Rounds are independent: a crash at 1.00× doesn't make a big one "due". Waiting a random number of rounds spreads your bets out over time; it doesn't change the odds of any bet.

    Getting started

    1. Open the game window and log in to the casino. The login is remembered.
    2. Set your plan on the Play tab. Start with a dry run: it watches real rounds and bets on paper.
    3. Try practice to see the bot place bets with play money.
    4. When you're ready, subscribe and choose Live.

    While it plays

    • Keep the game window open and visible. Don't minimise it.
    • Leave the first (left) bet panel alone, and don't bet with the second one: the bot reads its messages.
    • Stop waits for a bet in the air to settle. Auto Cash Out runs on the game's server, so a bet still cashes out even if your PC or internet drops.
    • If your balance runs low, the bot pauses. You can add funds and it carries on within your loss limit. Or just press Stop.

    If something goes wrong

    • "Waiting for Aviator": log in and open Aviator in the game window; the bot finds it by itself.
    • The bot stopped finding the game: the casino may have changed its page. Updates usually arrive automatically; check the website or contact support.
    • Payment approved but no subscription: press Restore purchase on the Subscription tab after a minute.

    Play responsibly

    ChainPilot can't predict rounds or beat the house edge. Only bet money you can afford to lose. Set a loss limit and a time limit, and take breaks.
